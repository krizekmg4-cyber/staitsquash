Show match dates and times in Eastern Time regardless of the viewer's device timezone. Use America/New_York so daylight saving is handled, not a fixed UTC offset.

**Why:** The user explicitly changed the requirement back to Eastern Time after previously requesting Pacific Time. The newer instruction supersedes the Pacific display requirement.

**How to apply:** Keep staff, coach and player match clocks, date labels, previous-time notices, and match-edit inputs consistent. Convert edited Eastern wall times back to absolute instants; never shift stored schedules or tournament import timezone settings merely to change their display.
